ChatNote Privacy Policy

Publisher: Pariyakorn Phasuk

Contact: xundund@icloud.com

Effective date: 4 November 2026

This policy describes how the ChatNote iOS app handles your information.

Your notes and settings

ChatNote stores the content you create or import, including chat and group names, note text, photos, drafts, unfinished edits, timestamps, and states such as pinned, done, locked, or deleted. It uses this information to display and organize your notes, search them, restore deleted items, and provide backup and sync features.

Your content is stored on your device. Appearance, text-size, and sorting preferences are also stored locally. ChatNote does not require a separate ChatNote account.

iCloud sync

When iCloud sync is available for ChatNote, the app syncs its content through Apple's CloudKit service to your private iCloud database, so your devices signed in to the same iCloud account can access it. This includes drafts, unfinished edits, and items retained in the Bin. If sync is unavailable, the app can continue to store notes locally.

ChatNote does not send your content to a separate server operated by the publisher. Your private CloudKit records are not visible to the publisher through Apple's developer portal. Apple provides the iCloud service under its own terms and privacy policy. You can manage ChatNote's iCloud access and storage through your device's Apple Account and iCloud settings.

Photos, camera, and authentication

ChatNote requests photo-library access when you use its photo browser, and camera access when you take a photo for a note. It uses those permissions to show photos you can choose and attach the photos you select or capture. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings and continue using text notes without them.

If you use chat locking, iOS verifies your identity using Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode, as available. ChatNote receives the authentication result; it does not receive your facial data, fingerprints, or passcode.

A chat lock controls access within the app. It does not add separate encryption to the chat or to exported backup files. Chat names can remain visible even when their contents are locked.

Widgets

To provide widgets, ChatNote saves a small snapshot in storage shared by the app and its widget extension on your device. The snapshot contains chat identifiers, names, lock states, and recent note text from unlocked chats, together with done marks and whether a note has a photo. It does not contain the photos themselves or unfinished drafts and edits.

Locked chats are included without their note text. Their names can still appear in widget selection and displays. Widgets may display unlocked note content on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, depending on the widgets you add and your device settings.

Backups you export or restore

Exporting creates a readable JSON file containing your active chats, notes, photos, drafts, and unfinished edits. Items in the Bin are excluded. Exporting locked chats requires device authentication, but the resulting file is not encrypted or password-protected by ChatNote.

You choose where to save or share the file. Anyone who obtains it can read its contents. The destination or service you choose handles the file under its own policies. Restoring a backup adds its chats to the app; it does not replace existing chats.

Retention and deletion

ChatNote keeps your content until you remove it. Deleting a chat or note first moves it to the Bin, where it remains until you restore it or permanently delete it. There is no automatic Bin expiration. Notes belonging to locked chats remain hidden from the Bin and are skipped when you empty it until the chat is unlocked.

To permanently remove content from the app, delete it from the Bin or use Empty and confirm. With iCloud sync available, those changes are synchronized to your other devices as syncing completes. Removing a group keeps its chats.

Deleting content in ChatNote does not delete backup files you previously exported or copies you shared. Remove those copies separately. Uninstalling the app also does not by itself ensure that iCloud data or device backups have been removed; manage those through Apple's settings and services.

Advertising and analytics

ChatNote does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and it does not send usage analytics to a publisher-operated server. Apple's platform services may process information under Apple's policies and your device and account settings.

Questions

For questions about ChatNote's handling of your information, use the contact information at the top of this policy. Your notes are managed on your device and in your private iCloud storage; use the in-app deletion controls and Apple's storage settings to manage those copies.
